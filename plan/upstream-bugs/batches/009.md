## #121 — Middle/side-button back does not work over the video surface

The issue thread specifies that middle/side-button back works outside the video area but fails over the video; the maintainer attributes this to player gesture changes. In target `Controls/PlayerControl.xaml.cs:2163-2174`, the player pointer handler sends pointer-down to gesture recognition and sets `e.Handled = true`. This prevents the bubbled `MainPage.Content_PointerPressed` handler (`MainPage.xaml.cs:134-168`) from receiving the event where middle/XButton1 back navigation is otherwise handled.

The pinned sibling fixes this exact route in `Controls/PlayerControl.xaml.cs:2375-2398`: it checks middle/XButton1 presses and calls `MessageCenter.GoBack(this)` before passing other presses to the gesture recognizer. The target snapshot still has the failure; it is a source-validated input-routing defect.
